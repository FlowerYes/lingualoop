# LinguaLoop

Mobile-first Spanish immersion through short vertical videos. Adult learners turn familiar scrolling into contextual vocabulary learning, with difficulty adjusting from watch behavior. The success path is the 28-step journey in the supplied brief.

Platform: responsive web, Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, Lucide. On mobile the active video fills the available viewport; desktop centers a 390-440px player. Onboarding, Feed, Learn and You are the only product surfaces.

Original brief commitments (feed appearance and dataset superseded by the September 12 follow-up below): original identity; dark video canvas, warm off-white supporting surfaces, one strong accent, large confident typography, rounded floating controls, subtle useful glass. Premium and calm, never childish. Doomersion's published iPhone screens and demo are the comparison bar only; do not copy its branding or assets.

Persistence is device-local. No auth, database, ingestion, social infrastructure or payment. AI is server-only through lib/ai.ts with deterministic fallback. At least twelve Spanish records at levels 1-4, synchronized clickable subtitles, saved words, three-question review, adaptive feedback, persistent stats and reset.

User authorized autonomous execution on 2026-09-12. The supplied detailed brief substitutes for further product/design interviews. Assumption: English is the learner's supporting language. Demo media will be transparently documented if synthetic or staged.
